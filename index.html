    Position Tips

    Position rotates each hand along with the blinds. Early positions act first and should play tighter ranges.

    • UTG/UTG+1: Tight range, avoid speculative hands.
    • Middle Position: You can open slightly wider, but stay disciplined.
    • Hijack/Cutoff: Great for steals and pressure.
    • Button: Best position for aggression and wide opens.
    • Small/Big Blind: Defend selectively; you act out of position post-flop.

    Poker Basics

    Table Positions

    Positions rotate each hand. The button acts last post-flop. Blinds are forced bets and act first after the flop.

    Betting Streets

    Pre-flop → Flop (3 cards) → Turn (1 card) → River (1 card). Each street has a betting round.

    Why Position Matters

    Acting last gives more information. Wider ranges are strongest from late position.

    Starting Hand Strength Guide

    Use this at a glance to understand which hands are strongest. Premium pairs and suited Broadway cards perform best.

    Premium: AA, KK, QQ, JJ, AKs
    Strong: TT, 99, AQs, AJs, KQs
    Playable: 88-22, suited connectors, suited aces
    Speculative: suited gappers, broadway offsuit
